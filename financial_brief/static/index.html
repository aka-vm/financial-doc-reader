Understand your financial documents

Turn reports, filings and deal documents into a readable brief or a checked ABS deal report.

What does Ledger do?

Financial brief: reads PDFs, annual reports, proxy filings (DEF 14A), HTML, text and CSV documents. It extracts key figures and statements with source quotations, writes a short summary, highlights risks, and lets you ask follow-up questions about the uploaded material.

ABS deal report: reads a folder or ZIP of US auto-loan securitization documents. It checks monthly servicer figures and balance carryovers, reads the contract’s payment waterfall and compares payments, and calculates loan-tape statistics to reconcile with reported pool balances. Results that cannot be confirmed appear under Needs review.

Source matching and arithmetic checks help identify problems; they do not guarantee that a document or interpretation is correct. Scanned PDFs need OCR first. Export JSON, Markdown or figures CSV, or save a PDF.

Documents are sent to the configured model provider. Uploaded originals are deleted after processing; results expire after one hour. Two reports can run at once. Additional uploads wait in a queue.
