ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — FICTIONAL COMPANY
Northstar Manufacturing — Annual financial summary for the year ended 31 December 2025

Revenue was USD 120 million in 2025, compared with USD 100 million in 2024.
Operating profit was USD 18 million in 2025, compared with USD 16 million in 2024.
Net income was USD 12 million in 2025, compared with USD 11 million in 2024.
Operating cash flow was USD 8 million in 2025, compared with USD 14 million in 2024.
Capital expenditure was USD 10 million in 2025.
Cash and cash equivalents were USD 9 million at 31 December 2025.
Total debt was USD 35 million at 31 December 2025.
Of this debt, USD 15 million matures in September 2026.
Management attributed the reduction in operating cash flow to higher inventory and slower customer collections.
The largest customer accounted for 32% of annual revenue in 2025.
The credit agreement requires a minimum cash balance of USD 5 million.
The company reported that it complied with this requirement at 31 December 2025.
Management expects to seek refinancing before the September 2026 debt maturity; no refinancing agreement had been signed as of the report date.
