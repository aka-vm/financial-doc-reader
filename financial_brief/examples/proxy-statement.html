Juniper Systems — Annual meeting proxy

For 2025, CEO total compensation was USD 4.2 million, consisting of USD 0.7 million salary, USD 0.5 million cash incentive and USD 3.0 million stock awards. These figures are grant-date accounting values, not realized pay.

For 2024, CEO total compensation was USD 3.8 million.

Seven of the nine directors were independent in 2025. Class B shareholders controlled 62% of voting power in 2025. The compensation vote is advisory. The audit committee approved a related-party lease; the disclosure does not quantify its annual cost.
