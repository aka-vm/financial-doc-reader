Deal Report Reader

Drop in a deal's documents: servicer reports, contracts, loan tapes, a .zip of them, or an EDGAR link. You get one report where every figure either passes the checks or is flagged with the reason.

What it does and how to use it
What you get
  • Deal at a glance: pool balance, notes outstanding, overcollateralization, reserve, losses, delinquency.
  • Notes: each class's balance, interest and principal for the month.
  • Payment waterfall: the payment order from the contract, with amounts confirmed against the servicer report.
  • Loan pool: loans, rates, credit scores and delinquency from the loan tape.
  • Needs review: anything that failed a check, with the reason. Everything else is verified.
How to use it
  1. Drop in documents of one auto-loan ABS deal: monthly servicer reports, the deal contracts (indenture, sale and servicing agreement), loan tapes, or a .zip. Or paste an EDGAR link, or a trust's CIK.
  2. Press Build report. One servicer report takes about a minute; a whole deal with contracts a few minutes.
  3. Read the report, then download it as JSON or print it to PDF.

Use it inside Claude

The same reports, from a chat. In Claude (web or mobile): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, and paste this link. Then ask, for example, "Build a deal report for CIK 2003263" or "Using CIK 2003263, are losses on this deal rising?". Claude builds the report here and walks you through it.
